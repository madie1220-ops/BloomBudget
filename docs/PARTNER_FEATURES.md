# BloomBudget Partner Features\n\n## Overview\n\nPartner features allow users to link their accounts with partners (spouses, roommates, friends) and share budgeting details securely. This enables collaborative financial planning and transparency.\n\n## Key Features\n\n### 1. Account Linking\n- Send partnership invitations via email\n- Accept or reject partnership requests\n- View all active partnerships\n- Remove partnerships at any time\n- One-way or mutual partnerships supported\n\n### 2. Budget Sharing\n- Share specific budgets with partners\n- Control sharing permissions:\n  - **View Only**: Partner can see budget details but cannot make changes\n  - **Edit**: Partner can modify budget amounts and view transactions\n  - **Full**: Partner has complete control (edit, delete, share further)\n- Share transactions visibility\n- Remove shared budgets\n\n### 3. Partnership Status\n- **Pending**: Awaiting partner acceptance\n- **Accepted**: Active partnership with shared access\n- **Rejected**: Partnership invitation declined\n- **Blocked**: Partnership suspended\n\n## API Endpoints\n\n### Partnership Management\n\n#### Send Partner Invitation\n```\nPOST /api/partners/invite\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\nContent-Type: application/json\n\n{\n  \"partnerEmail\": \"partner@example.com\"\n}\n\nResponse:\n{\n  \"message\": \"Partnership invitation sent\",\n  \"partnership\": {\n    \"_id\": \"partnership_id\",\n    \"userId\": \"your_user_id\",\n    \"partnerId\": \"partner_user_id\",\n    \"status\": \"pending\",\n    \"sharedBudgets\": [],\n    \"sharedTransactions\": true\n  }\n}\n```\n\n#### Get Pending Requests\n```\nGET /api/partners/requests\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\n\nResponse:\n[\n  {\n    \"_id\": \"request_id\",\n    \"userId\": {\n      \"_id\": \"user_id\",\n      \"name\": \"John Doe\",\n      \"email\": \"john@example.com\"\n    },\n    \"status\": \"pending\",\n    \"createdAt\": \"2024-01-15T10:30:00Z\"\n  }\n]\n```\n\n#### Accept Partnership\n```\nPOST /api/partners/{partnerId}/accept\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\n\nResponse:\n{\n  \"message\": \"Partnership accepted\",\n  \"partnership\": {...}\n}\n```\n\n#### Reject Partnership\n```\nPOST /api/partners/{partnerId}/reject\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\n\nResponse:\n{\n  \"message\": \"Partnership rejected\",\n  \"partnership\": {...}\n}\n```\n\n#### Get Active Partnerships\n```\nGET /api/partners\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\n\nResponse:\n[\n  {\n    \"_id\": \"partnership_id\",\n    \"userId\": {...},\n    \"partnerId\": {...},\n    \"status\": \"accepted\",\n    \"sharedBudgets\": [\"budget_id_1\", \"budget_id_2\"],\n    \"sharedTransactions\": true\n  }\n]\n```\n\n#### Remove Partner\n```\nDELETE /api/partners/{partnerId}\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\n\nResponse:\n{\n  \"message\": \"Partnership removed\"\n}\n```\n\n### Budget Sharing\n\n#### Share Budget with Partner\n```\nPOST /api/partners/budgets/share\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\nContent-Type: application/json\n\n{\n  \"budgetId\": \"budget_id\",\n  \"partnerId\": \"partner_id\",\n  \"permissions\": \"edit\" // 'view', 'edit', or 'full'\n}\n\nResponse:\n{\n  \"message\": \"Budget shared successfully\",\n  \"sharedBudget\": {\n    \"_id\": \"shared_budget_id\",\n    \"budgetId\": \"budget_id\",\n    \"ownerId\": \"your_user_id\",\n    \"sharedWithUserId\": \"partner_id\",\n    \"permissions\": \"edit\"\n  }\n}\n```\n\n#### Get Shared Budgets\n```\nGET /api/partners/budgets/shared\nAuthorization: Bearer <token>\n\nResponse:\n[\n  {\n    \"_id\": \"shared_budget_id\",\n    \"budgetId\": {...},\n    \"ownerId\": {...},\n    \"sharedWithUserId\": {...},\n    \"permissions\": \"edit\",\n    \"createdAt\": \"2024-01-15T10:30:00Z\"\n  }\n]\n```\n\n## Use Cases\n\n### Couples / Married Partners\n- Link accounts to track combined household expenses\n- Share family budgets for transparency\n- Collaborate on shared financial goals\n- Monitor individual spending categories\n\n### Roommates\n- Share rent/utilities budgets\n- Split expense tracking\n- Monitor shared household expenses\n- Transparent splitting of costs\n\n### Business Partners\n- Track business expenses collaboratively\n- Share project budgets\n- Monitor resource allocation\n- Maintain accountability\n\n## Security Considerations\n\n1. **Invitation System**: Only invited users can become partners\n2. **Permission Levels**: Granular control over what partners can see/edit\n3. **Audit Trail**: All budget modifications logged\n4. **Revocation**: Can immediately remove partner access\n5. **End-to-End**: Partners can have different subscription levels\n\n## Subscription Tier Limitations\n\n| Feature | Free | Pro | Premium |\n|---------|------|-----|----------|\n| Partners allowed | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |\n| Shared budgets | Limited | 10 | Unlimited |\n| Permission levels | View only | View, Edit | View, Edit, Full |\n| Shared transactions | No | Yes | Yes |\n| Family sharing | No | Yes | Yes |\n\n## Frontend Integration\n\nThe Partners page (/partners) provides:\n- Partner invitation form\n- Active partnerships list\n- Pending requests management\n- Shared budgets overview\n- Permission level indicators\n- Quick removal options\n\n## Error Handling\n\n### Common Errors\n\n- `User not found` - Email doesn't exist in system\n- `Cannot partner with yourself` - Self-partnership not allowed\n- `Partnership already exists` - Partnership pending or active\n- `Invalid permissions level` - Only 'view', 'edit', 'full' allowed\n- `Budget not found` - Budget doesn't exist or you don't own it\n- `Active partnership required` - Partnership must be accepted first\n\n## Best Practices\n\n1. **Start with View Only**: Begin with view-only permissions, upgrade to edit if needed\n2. **Regular Reviews**: Periodically review shared budgets\n3. **Clear Communication**: Discuss budget limits and sharing permissions upfront\n4. **Clean Up**: Remove partnerships that are no longer needed\n5. **Backup Important Data**: Export budget data before removing partnerships\n\n## Future Enhancements\n\n- Real-time notifications for partner budget changes\n- Shared budget comments/notes\n- Budget splitting and rebalancing\n- Automated expense sharing calculations\n- Partner activity history\n- Budget collaboration workflows\n"
